# References and what they support

Targeted checks from 21 October 2026 are recorded in the [maintenance review](review-2026-10-21.md). Earlier access dates and search records remain unchanged.

Long author lists use first author followed by “et al.” Publication details and source-level access limits are recorded in the [reference audit](reference-audit.json). The historical [rapid review](literature-review.md) documents discovery and implementation decisions; it is not an exhaustive systematic review.

## Research articles

- **Delaney JS (2004).** ESOL: estimating aqueous solubility directly from molecular structure. *Journal of Chemical Information and Computer Sciences* **44**(3):1000–1005. [DOI: 10.1021/ci034243x](https://doi.org/10.1021/ci034243x). [PMID: 15154768](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15154768/). Original model and data context, appraised from abstract/citation; this project does not reproduce its fitted equation.
- **Llompart P et al. (2024).** Will we ever be able to accurately predict solubility? *Scientific Data* **11**(1):303. [DOI: 10.1038/s41597-024-03105-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03105-6). [PMID: 38499581](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38499581/). Measurement definitions, overlapping data and external evaluation. Its performance estimates are not transferred.
- **Meng J et al. (2022).** Boosting the predictive performance with aqueous solubility dataset curation. *Scientific Data* **9**(1):71. [DOI: 10.1038/s41597-022-01154-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01154-3). [PMID: 35241693](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35241693/). Curation and repeated records affect the specific workflow studied; its filtering rules and numerical improvements are not adopted here.
- **Bemis GW, Murcko MA (1996).** The properties of known drugs. 1. Molecular frameworks. *Journal of Medicinal Chemistry* **39**(15):2887–2893. [DOI: 10.1021/jm9602928](https://doi.org/10.1021/jm9602928). [PMID: 8709122](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8709122/). Conceptual framework basis, appraised from abstract. Exact executable behavior is defined by the pinned RDKit source.
- **Wu Z et al. (2018).** MoleculeNet: a benchmark for molecular machine learning. *Chemical Science* **9**(2):513–530. [DOI: 10.1039/C7SC02664A](https://doi.org/10.1039/C7SC02664A). [PMID: 29629118](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29629118/). Benchmark context and processed Delaney task. Different features, partitions and model grids make its scores non-equivalent to this project’s results.
- **Guo Q, Hernandez-Hernandez S, Ballester PJ (2025).** UMAP-based clustering split for rigorous evaluation of AI models for virtual screening on cancer cell lines. *Journal of Cheminformatics* **17**(1):94. [DOI: 10.1186/s13321-025-01039-8](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-025-01039-8). [PMID: 40495205](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40495205/). Shows why different scaffold labels may retain structural similarity. Its endpoint is cell-line virtual screening, not solubility; neither effect sizes nor a preferred split are transferred.

## Data and software

- **Delaney JS (2004).** *ESOL: Estimating Aqueous Solubility Directly from Molecular Structure — supporting data*. ACS Figshare, item 7944677, version 1. [DOI: 10.1021/ci034243x.s001](https://doi.org/10.1021/ci034243x.s001). Source of matched records; the live metadata identifies CC BY-NC 4.0. Accessed 20 October 2026. See [data reuse terms](../data/LICENSE.md).
- **DeepChem maintainers.** [Official Delaney loader](https://github.com/deepchem/deepchem/blob/master/deepchem/molnet/load_function/delaney_datasets.py). Accessed 20 October 2026; observed file identity is in the audit. Supplies processed-data URL and target identity. Its software license is not the scientific-data license, and this repository does not execute its loader or featurizers.
- **RDKit developers.** [MurckoScaffold.py, Release_2025_09_1](https://github.com/rdkit/rdkit/blob/Release_2025_09_1/rdkit/Chem/Scaffolds/MurckoScaffold.py). Official source for the runtime pinned to RDKit 2025.9.1. Accessed 20 October 2026. The [current API page](https://www.rdkit.org/docs/source/rdkit.Chem.Scaffolds.MurckoScaffold.html) identifies itself as 2026.09.1 and is supplementary, not documentation of the recorded runtime. The ordinary nonchiral scaffold function used here is distinct from `MakeScaffoldGeneric`.
- **scikit-learn developers.** [GroupKFold](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.GroupKFold.html), [GroupShuffleSplit](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.GroupShuffleSplit.html), and [common pitfalls](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/common_pitfalls.html), version 1.8.0. Official documentation, accessed 20 October 2026. Supports group separation, group-fraction semantics and training-only preprocessing. It cannot establish that a chemical grouping captures every dependency.
- **scikit-learn developers.** [Mean squared error](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/generated/sklearn.metrics.mean_squared_error.html), [mean absolute error](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/generated/sklearn.metrics.mean_absolute_error.html), and [R²](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/generated/sklearn.metrics.r2_score.html), version 1.8.0. Official documentation, accessed 20 October 2026. Supports the independent arithmetic contract. The repository deliberately records undefined constant-target R² as `null`.

A correct arithmetic result or a matching split does not certify the measurements, guarantee performance elsewhere, or identify the unique fitted model that generated a prediction vector.
