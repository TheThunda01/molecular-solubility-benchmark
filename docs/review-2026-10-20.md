# Scientific and editorial review — 20 October 2026

This review covered the README, authored guides, data and license notices, source records, saved reports, split audit and prediction example. The relevant data reader, descriptor definitions, splitting, model selection and verification code were inspected against the documentation. This is a correction and source-verification pass, not a new performance study.

## Corrections and clarifications

- Corrected the contributing guide’s instruction to exclude all raw measurements from development. Models should use designated training measurements; held-out outcomes and source-fitted predictions must stay out of fitting, preprocessing and tuning decisions.
- Replaced numbered placeholder citations in the chemistry guide with linked author–year references. Standardized article metadata, added verified PMIDs and issue identifiers, and corrected the incomplete Meng author listing to “Meng et al.” (the source lists eight authors).
- Distinguished the pinned RDKit 2025.9.1 implementation from the current 2026.09.1 API page. Linked the release-tagged scaffold source and explained that the ordinary nonchiral scaffold function is distinct from `MakeScaffoldGeneric`.
- Clarified that the random splitter targets a fraction of groups, not a guaranteed fraction of rows. Five inner folds describe this dataset; another valid input uses up to five, subject to its number of groups.
- Corrected “ordered-independent” to “order-independent” for set fingerprints; expanded metric, structure and measurement vocabulary in context and in the [glossary](glossary.md).
- Removed ambiguity about completed features: frozen prediction export/verification are implemented; repeated partitions, external data and standalone split-audit verification remain future work.
- Added an [assumption register](assumptions.md) explaining measurement heterogeneity, duplicate retention, identity conventions, descriptor choice, the 80/20 target, acyclic grouping, fold weighting, model grids and descriptive similarity cutoffs. These design choices are not presented as universally validated settings.

## Source coverage

The [reference audit](reference-audit.json) contains 16 records: six research articles, one dataset and nine official software/documentation resources. PubMed metadata and abstracts were checked for all six articles. Complete PMC bodies were retrieved in paginated portions for Llompart, Meng, Wu and Guo, with relevant measurement, curation and evaluation sections appraised. Delaney and Bemis–Murcko remained abstract-only; neither was described as a full-text review.

The live ACS Figshare API confirmed item 7944677, supporting-data DOI, file identity and CC BY-NC 4.0 license. The official DeepChem loader confirmed the processed-data location and target. The pinned RDKit scaffold source and versioned scikit-learn group/metric documentation were checked. Different-task findings from Guo et al. support inspecting structural similarity, not transferring a numerical effect size to solubility.

## Verification

All 49 existing tests passed with the pinned dependencies. The separate verifier checked the stored example’s 1,356 held-out predictions and all six baseline metric rows. Local heading, link, anchor and source-audit checks are recorded in the portfolio review report. No code, measured data, split assignment, fitted configuration, numerical result, figure or historical search record was changed.

The data’s 1,128 records and 1,117 canonical identities, the two 902/226 partitions and the 1,356 prediction rows remain the recorded contracts. Passing arithmetic and membership checks does not authenticate a particular generating estimator or establish measurement validity.

## Remaining limits

No external validation or new interval-calibration study was performed. Unknown assay conditions and the undocumented reason for the 16-record difference between source and processed selection remain explicit. The different test-set compositions prevent a causal attribution of their score difference solely to scaffold overlap. Frozen result, audit and prediction-example reports were reviewed and preserved, with current explanations linked from the README. This review improves accuracy and accessibility without claiming professional deployment readiness or that every possible error has been eliminated.
